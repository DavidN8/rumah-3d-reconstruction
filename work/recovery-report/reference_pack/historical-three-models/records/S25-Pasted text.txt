## BUG 1: The Model Is Upside Down (`09_merge_export.py` Gravity & Up-Vector Bug)
**What ZCode did wrong:**
- In `09_merge_export.py`, ZCode assumed the native COLMAP world frame was `+Z up` and applied a wrapper node flipping "native Z-up to glTF Y-up".
- In reality, `IMG_0180.MOV` was extracted as upright portrait frames (`1080×1920`). In COLMAP/OpenCV camera convention, camera `+X` is right, **`+Y` is DOWN**, and `+Z` is forward! Therefore, in the COLMAP world frame, the average camera `-Y` axis points UP toward the ceiling, while `+Y` points DOWN into the floor!
**How to fix it mathematically (and verify it visually!):**
1. Compute the true **World Up Vector ($\vec{u}_{\text{up}}$)** directly from the 501 registered cameras in `colmap-gpu/merged/sparse/0`:
   - For each camera $i$ with world-to-camera rotation $R_i$, the camera's local "up" direction (toward the top of the portrait 1080×1920 frame) in world coordinates is $\vec{u}_i = R_i^T \begin{bmatrix} 0 & -1 & 0 \end{bmatrix}^T$.
   - Average $\vec{u}_i$ across the upright walking frames and normalize to get $\vec{u}_{\text{up}}$, and cross-check it against the floor-to-ceiling normal vector from the floor/ceiling planes in the mesh (RANSAC plane fit on the lowest/highest vertices along $\vec{u}_{\text{up}}$: floor normals must point in $+\vec{u}_{\text{up}}$, and camera centers must sit ~1.3m–1.6m ABOVE the floor plane, NOT below it!).
2. Construct a clean, proper rotation matrix $R_{\text{gltf}} \in SO(3)$ (`det(R) = +1`, NEVER a reflection `det = -1` that turns triangles inside-out!) that maps $\vec{u}_{\text{up}} \mapsto \begin{bmatrix} 0 & +1 & 0 \end{bmatrix}^T$ (glTF standard `+Y up`), levels the floor plane horizontally ($Y = 0$ at the floor), and centers the apartment at $(X=0, Z=0)$.
3. **Bake this rigid transform directly into the vertex positions and vertex normals** of the exported GLB (or apply it consistently to both mesh vertices and exported camera poses in `cameras.json` / `detail-models.json`), rather than relying on a broken wrapper node!
4. **Mandatory Verification:** Render a front/isometric PNG and a side-elevation PNG (`Y` axis vertical) of the transformed mesh showing the floor at $Y = 0$, walls going up to $Y \approx +2.5\text{ m}$, and the kitchen counter / bed / furniture sitting **right-side up on the floor**!
