Depth, camera and seed contract
The fresh MoGe maps use a 598 x 1064 grid. Focal lengths and principal point are
scaled from the original 1080 x 1920 calibration separately in x and y. Seed RGB is
sampled on the same grid.
Depth is explicit millimetre z-depth. Each reliable map is robustly aligned to this
image's associated, observed sparse tracks in the transformed component frame.
Unreliable or non-positive fits are omitted from depth supervision and seeding; RGB
and normals remain available.
Proper rotations have determinant +1. Geometry and all 501 camera poses share
the same component-level similarity. The final matrices and projected sparse tracks
are checked independently of screenshots.
The legacy single-K seed entry point rejects a merged, two-calibration model. The
retired zone-0/1/2 commands cannot silently overwrite the component
reconstruction.
Native recipe: merge 0.5, default grid 512, four bisection iterations, carve-k 3, hole ratio 0, hole
edge limit 64, floater minimum 50 faces, 8192 texture and JPEG90. The edge rule still fills small
holes even when the ratio is zero; no manual wall patch was added.

sanity check, not an independent measurement of true surfaces.

Issues and struggles
- Frame 1398 still contains torn wall geometry inside the unified living component.
Restoring all usable views improves the door but does not fully solve the wall.
- Kitchen-front floor gaps, cabinet-side perforations, softened small objects and a
few bedroom wall patches remain in the native extraction.
- The kitchen join remains approximate: the wider bridge did not provide three
orthogonal distributed constraints, and the candidate transform was rejected.
- Native geometry preservation and zero newly empty pixels prove delivery integrity,
not full source fidelity, independent generalization or surveyed dimensional
accuracy.
Recommended next direction
- Keep the published full native reconstruction as the primary baseline. Do not
reintroduce count/byte-driven decimation unless the user later requests it.
- Target the remaining doorway defect with a bounded extraction/occupancy or
depth/normal-supervision ablation, judged from the same source cameras. Preserve
the current native reference.
- Do not reuse the temporal bedroom split, local bedroom tilt, nearest-camera crop
or an unsupported wall patch. Any inferred geometric repair should be explicit and
separately reviewable.
- If precise kitchen registration is required, use the existing unused doorway footage
and line/plane constraints. The weak wall-only bridge should remain rejected.

settings or depth/normal supervision?
