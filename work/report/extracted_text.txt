disabled. Restoring faces reverses this regression; it does not cure the underlying
warped/porous reconstruction.

08 / COORDINATE CONTRACT
One transform for geometry and its cameras
Correcting a display frame fixes orientation and numerical consistency. It does not fix source geometry or validate room dimensions.
GEMINI ARGON / ARCHITECT  |  27 OCT 2026  |  Evidence-based handoff
12
Observed frame, not an assumption
Averaging camera local-up transformed to world coordinates gives approximately
[0.0047, 0.0181, 0.9998]: the original world was already close to +Z-up. Camera
+Y-down conventions alone do not identify world gravity. The original display
mapping sent native +Z toward glTF -Y. [S03]
Current export mapping
For each zone, bake p' = s R p + t into positions; rotate normals by R. Rotations are
proper (det R approximately +1). Fitted local floor planes map to Y = 0; XZ
centering is shared with camera output. No root orientation wrapper remains.
[S02-S04]
Cameras use the same similarity: C' = s R C + t; world-to-camera rotation Rcv' = Rcv
R transpose. glTF camera orientation also accounts for the CV-to-viewer axis
convention. Numerical projection invariance is below 8 x 10^-10 pixels. This
verifies the conversion, not camera accuracy. [S04]
Training coordinates remain frozen
Preserved input
zones/zone-0, zone-1, zone-2 and their existing checkpoints still use the
original training frame. Re-extraction reads those frozen assets.
Explicit correction
analysis/fixed-transforms.json defines per-zone transformations. The merger
bakes them into the derivative mesh and creates matching corrected
cameras.
Corrected output
colmap-gpu/merged/sparse/0 and corrected/sparse/0 carry a FRAME.json
contract. New preprocessing is directed to upright datasets to prevent mixing
old checkpoints with new poses.
Architectural concern: local floor leveling includes a separate zone-2 adjustment.
The export is not one unchanged rigid transform of the entire original
living/bedroom component. Cross-zone continuity still requires visual and
geometric validation.

11 / UNRESOLVED PIPELINE ISSUE
Depth preparation was corrected; the model was not retrained
A code fix to future input preparation is not a repair to existing checkpoints. This is a key limitation for the next architect.
GEMINI ARGON / ARCHITECT  |  27 OCT 2026  |  Evidence-based handoff
15
Two separate depth problems
The original MoGe PNGs were relative, normalized per image. They cannot be
interpreted directly as millimetres. I generated a separate 76-frame metric subset
using explicit mm output and z-depth convention; it supports the seam estimate
without overwriting training inputs. [S10, S27;
implementation/14_metric_bridge.py]
In the seed-cloud preparation I found resized depth (about 598 x 1064) paired
with full-resolution 1080 x 1920 intrinsics and RGB sampling. The code now scales
intrinsics and color sampling consistently to the depth grid, and guards frame
selection. Existing seed clouds and trained splats were not regenerated.
[implementation/05_seed_cloud.py]
Implication for diagnosis
The old initialization may have contributed to poor surfaces. The report does
not claim how much: that requires a controlled pilot with corrected inputs
and the same held-out evaluation.
What the remaining artifacts suggest
Folded walls, overlapping sheets, damaged small objects and ghost surfaces
appear in current source comparisons. These may involve camera/zone
inconsistency, weak initialization, learned-depth error, transient content and
splat-to-mesh extraction. The current evidence does not isolate one universal
cause.
Why more triangles is not enough
The 11.64M source mesh is more complete than the compact one in the ablation,
but already contains reconstruction defects. The 8K texture bakes appearance
onto whatever surface exists. Increasing iterations or filling holes could preserve
or hide an incorrect surface unless geometry is checked independently.
Recommended controlled test: one fixed kitchen/doorway subset; corrected
depth-grid intrinsics; explicit depth units; verified cameras; frozen random
seed/config; a held-out photo set and matched novel viewpoints. Compare
original extraction, compact extraction and an error-bounded simplification of the
original.

17 / ISSUES, STRUGGLES AND LESSONS
What did not work, and what the evidence actually says
Failed experiments are retained as evidence; they are not presented as validated solutions.
GEMINI ARGON / ARCHITECT  |  27 OCT 2026  |  Evidence-based handoff
21
A shortcut for zone ownership
Closest camera plus frustum inclusion ignored wall occlusion and deleted valid
surfaces. Fixed by disabling broad default cuts; no safe automatic ownership
replacement has yet been validated.
A misleading scale story
Walking-speed ratios and an initially wrong floor/countertop datum made the
original kitchen-scale explanation unreliable. The current correction is an estimate
with explicit uncertainty.
Weak registration witnesses
Sparse-track and optical-flow bridges failed. Learned matches yielded only one
localized planar patch; low fit residual cannot support a high-confidence
whole-room claim.
Parameter experiments without a quality win
Hybrid extraction settings increased geometry in some zones but produced larger
floor gaps and did not repair the bedroom. These candidates were rejected and
not promoted.
Correctness checks missed usefulness
A consistent coordinate transform, a smaller GLB and fast controls all passed while
the mesh looked bad. These checks must stay separate from source-based surface
acceptance.
A real preprocessing fix remains unapplied
The corrected depth-grid intrinsics/color sampling protect future builds. Existing
seed clouds and checkpoints still embody the previous preparation; their quality
cannot improve merely because the script changed.
