Read-only architectural-plane feasibility and v4 comparison
31 October 2026

The data support local plane regularization as a possible later experiment,
especially on the higher white ceiling. They do not support automatic completion
of whole wall rectangles. v4 improves waviness on one well-supported wall family
and improves coverage, while other wall regions remain mixed. No geometry was
changed and no additional reconstruction trial was started for this audit.

Evidence and frame

Used the accepted normalized Y-up main SfM component, with the strict reader for
OPENCV_FISHEYE model 5 / 8 parameters. Rechecked 25,022 accepted points against
154,411 observations: each point has at least 3 masked observations, reprojection
error <= 2 px and fisheye theta <= 85 degrees. The zero writer point.error field was
ignored. Baseline is osmo-lite-trusted-source.glb; v4 is recovery-v2/v4-probe.glb.
Source hashes are recorded in the JSON reports. Both meshes remain unchanged.

Distances are approximate scene units converted to centimetres using the assumed
1.5 m median camera-height normalization. This is not measured metric ground truth.
The audit was CPU-only, with four linear-algebra threads and process affinity
restricted to at most eight logical CPUs.

Stable support

Wall candidate 4: 1,131 tracks, 139 source images, 5.64 approximate square metres of
convex-hull support; spatial-half plane-normal difference 0.25 degrees. Baseline
nearby parallel surfaces have median/p90 plane error 2.78/9.17 cm, with 76.8% within
5 cm. v4 changes those to 2.32/9.19 cm and 77.3%. The broad p90 includes different
surface coverage and nearby layers; it alone does not measure waviness fairly.

Higher ceiling candidate 12: Y approximately 2.321; 307 tracks, 1,432 observations,
122 source images; 7.18 approximate square metres of hull support. Spatial-half
normal difference 0.56 degrees. Baseline median/p90 error 1.59/3.16 cm; 98.75% of the
conditional nearby parallel surface samples lie within 5 cm. v4 median/p90 becomes
2.32/3.60 cm; 98.1% remains within 5 cm. These are strong local plane hypotheses,
not proof that every blank area of their convex hull is a real wall/ceiling.

Fixed common-grid comparison

Used identical 5 cm grid cells, identical plane equations, excluded cells crossed
by at least 2 distinct trusted background-feature rays, and required both meshes
to have a normal-ray hit within 20 cm. Subtracted each surface's signed median to
separate offset from waviness. Neighbor differences use the same valid grid edges.

Candidates 4 and 7 are nearby hypotheses from the same wall family; they are not
two independent demonstrations on separate rooms. The fixed-grid evidence does
show locally smoother v4 geometry. It does not justify saying all white walls
are now flat. Common-cell shape measurements omit newly recovered coverage,
which is reported separately. Wall 4 missing near-plane grid cells improve
926 -> 846; higher ceiling 610 -> 599. Some other walls gain little or regress.

Why blanket filling is unsafe

On baseline wall 4, 194 of 926 missing cells have at least 2 trusted background
features observed through the proposed plane. On several other wall candidates,
roughly half or more of the missing cells have such negative evidence. These
can be real doors, windows, finite wall boundaries or visible space behind a
surface. A rectangular planar cap would close legitimate openings. Absence of
a negative witness is not positive evidence that a surface exists.

The higher ceiling has 240 gap cells within 10 cm of sparse plane support and no
negative witness. Only 30 have visibility in two of the six upright reference
photos. Those 30 have bright neutral colors, with median cross-view 5x5 RGB-mean
standard deviation about 2/255. This supports their appearance, but white paint
has too little texture for RGB agreement alone to prove the proposed geometry.
Most candidates lack enough overlap in those six reference views. The raw 244
calibrated fisheye frames would be needed for stronger local validation.

Recommended direction

Keep the current v4 geometry/texture review and deployment work as planned.
Do not add an automatic room-wide plane-fill step to this delivery. The evidence
supports describing better local wall smoothness and coverage, with remaining
holes and uneven walls explicitly acknowledged.

If a later local repair experiment is warranted, start with small, genuinely
closed boundary loops on the stable ceiling/wall regions. Require a small loop
diameter, near-planar boundary, normal agreement, nearby sparse support, and no
background-ray contradiction; protect door/window edges with an expanded mask.
The earlier topology audit counted 173 loops with diameter <= 5 cm and plane max
error <= 5 mm across the entire baseline, before these additional evidence gates.
That is a candidate pool, not an approved repair count. Prefer several separated
raw viewpoints and compare a separate candidate against the original geometry,
source photos and ray consistency. Do not bridge open boundary chains or use the
convex hull as a completion mask. No such repair was performed here.
